Pick a class and its perks, as on the class selection screen: that gives the lance or spear, the horse and any speed perk. Each graph shows a full thrust from horseback against a player at every armor level, with the mod (solid) and in vanilla (dashed), by closing speed: how fast attacker and target move toward each other along the thrust. Dots mark the horse's top speed, a full-gallop hit into a standing target. Drag across a graph to zoom in, double-click to zoom back out.

Hit

Armor is the class's armor value (the same on every body part in multiplayer). Weapons with a changed base damage (shown as vanilla+change) compare with the vanilla weapon, so they can beat vanilla at low speed. Real hits vary with how far the thrust had extended, the angle and the exact speeds.

Numbers come from the mod's current defaults and the game's data, using the same formulas as the server (vanilla's thrust, the mod's speed curves and weapon changes, multiplayer armor).
